ITR Methods attribution

SCAF
Haixu Ma, Donglin Zeng, and Yufeng Liu (2022).
Learning Individualized Treatment Rules with Many Treatments: A Supervised
Clustering Approach Using Adaptive Fusion. NeurIPS 35, 15956–15969.
This package implements the squared-loss SCAF method in Python. Its numerical
solver is a new implementation. The original R and C++ sources are archived
separately from the distribution.

NSCF
Daixi Huang, Yixin Yuan, Yifan Dai, and Yufeng Liu (2026).
Neural Supervised Clustering for Learning Individualized Treatment Rules with
Many Treatments. arXiv:2610.19104.
The NSCF implementation is from the private NSCF project.
